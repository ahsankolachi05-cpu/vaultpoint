Features

How it works

Up and running in four steps

A straightforward path from sign-up to your first international payment — no paperwork marathons, no jargon.

Create your account

Tell us about your business and verify your identity online. Most applications take minutes, not days.

Add money

Top up by bank transfer, or share your account details with clients so they can pay you directly.

Convert & pay

Convert between currencies at a clearly shown rate, and pay suppliers or staff — with the fee confirmed upfront.

Track everything

Follow every payment in real time, reconcile in your accounting tool, and review analytics whenever you need them.

Security & compliance

Designed to protect every pound

Security is treated as a product feature, not a footnote. These are the protective measures the platform is designed around.

Encryption everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, so account information and payment details are protected whether they are moving or stored.

Two-factor & biometric sign-in

Accounts are designed to support two-factor authentication and biometric sign-in on mobile, adding a second layer beyond your password.

Real-time monitoring

Transactions are monitored in real time for unusual activity, with instant alerts so you can react the moment something looks wrong.

Instant card freeze

Lost a card, or spotted a payment you don't recognise? Freeze any card instantly in the app — and unfreeze it just as quickly if it turns up.

A note on this page: Vaultpoint is a concept brand and this website is a design demonstration. The measures above describe how the platform is designed to work — they are not certifications, audit results or regulatory statements, and no financial services are offered here.
